Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 13, 2026

Note Duplicate Cleaner is developed by AI Workers Lab. The app is designed to process Apple Notes locally on the user's Mac.

Data collection

The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. It contains no account system, analytics SDK, advertising SDK, crash-reporting SDK, or remote backend.

Apple Notes access

With the user's macOS permission, the app reads note titles, bodies, identifiers, account and folder identifiers, and modification dates needed to detect exact duplicates and preserve their source locations. It can move confirmed duplicate notes to [NDC] Undo Queue and restore them.

Local storage

Scan results and the undo journal are stored locally in the app's sandbox container. The undo journal contains note identifiers and source folder identifiers needed for restoration. Note bodies are not stored in the undo journal.

Network use

The app does not send Apple Notes data or app usage data over the network.

Deletion and control

Users can remove scan history and completed undo records in the app. Moved notes remain under the user's control in Apple Notes. Deleting the app removes its sandbox data according to macOS behavior but does not delete the user's Apple Notes.

Apple services

Apple may process App Store purchases, downloads, diagnostics, or other platform information under Apple's own terms and privacy policy. AI Workers Lab does not receive Apple Notes content from Apple.

Changes and support

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated before the new behavior is released. Questions can be submitted through the support issue tracker.
