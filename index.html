Note Duplicate Cleaner

A conservative macOS utility that finds exact duplicate Apple Notes, keeps the oldest original, and moves extra copies to a restorable undo queue.

Get support Privacy policy

How it protects your notes

  • It matches only the same title and body after minimal representation normalization.
  • It preserves the oldest exact copy.
  • It moves extras to [NDC] Undo Queue instead of permanently deleting them.
  • It skips locked, shared, attachment-bearing, and unreadable notes.
  • It does not send note content or usage data over the network.

Getting started

  1. Open the app and allow macOS to automate Apple Notes.
  2. Choose all folders or a smaller set.
  3. Review every detected group before moving anything.
  4. Use the in-app undo queue if you need to restore a moved note.

Frequently asked questions

Does the app permanently delete notes?
No. Confirmed duplicates are moved into a dedicated Notes folder so they remain visible and restorable.

Why are some notes skipped?
The app deliberately avoids locked, shared, attachment-bearing, or unreadable notes because they need additional context and should not be handled automatically.

Where is my data processed?
On your Mac. The app has no account, backend, advertising, or analytics service.
